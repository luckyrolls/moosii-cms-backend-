These rules apply on top of the base instructions for income sub-segments.

## Subjects that work
Pay arriving and being routed: a plain envelope on a doormat, a laptop closed on a simple
work desk at the end of the day, a work bag and keys by the door, a single envelope
splitting into two neat trays on a shelf as a metaphor for splitting a deposit. Work can be
suggested by its ordinary tools — a lunchbox, a lanyard with a blank badge, a folded apron —
not by an office.

## Traps specific to this topic
- NO paychecks, pay stubs or bank statements with visible amounts; documents are blank.
- NO corporate imagery: no briefcases and skyscrapers, no handshakes, no suited executives.
- No money flowing through the air, no cash in hands.
