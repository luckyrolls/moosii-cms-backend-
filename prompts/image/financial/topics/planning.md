These rules apply on top of the base instructions for planning sub-segments.

## Subjects that work
Calm forethought: a wall calendar with blank squares and one square softly highlighted, an
open notebook and pen on a kitchen table in morning light, a coat and keys ready by the door,
a simple path or road seen from above leading off the frame.

## Traps specific to this topic
- Calendars, planners and notebooks are blank: no dates, no numbers, no writing.
- NO timelines, Gantt bars, flowcharts, checklists with ticks, or target-and-arrow icons.
- No crystal balls, hourglasses or clocks as symbols for the future.
