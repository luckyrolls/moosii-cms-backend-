These rules apply on top of the base instructions for planning sub-segments.

Planning cards are about CHOOSING ONE THING and getting ready for it. Choose the metaphor whose
meaning matches the card.

## Metaphors (options, not a checklist)
- **Ready the night before** — a bag packed by the front door, clothes over a chair back, a
  kettle and a single mug set out on a dark counter. Fits cards about preparing, making the
  next step easy, removing friction.
- **One thing chosen from many** — one coat taken from a full rail, a single chair pulled out
  from a table of four, one drawer open in a run of closed ones. Fits cards about picking a
  single priority, deciding what to do first.
- **A near path, not a map** — stepping stones across a small garden, a hallway with a door
  open at the end, a staircase turning out of sight with daylight above. Fits cards about
  sequencing, what comes after what, the next stretch rather than the whole journey.

## Traps specific to this topic
- Calendars, planners and notebooks are BLANK: no dates, no numbers, no writing. Prefer a place
  to a calendar — the calendar has become this set's default and reads as an icon.
- NO timelines, Gantt bars, flowcharts, tick-lists, target-and-arrow imagery.
- No crystal balls, hourglasses or clock faces standing in for the future.
