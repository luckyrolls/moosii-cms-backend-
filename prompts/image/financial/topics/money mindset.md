These rules apply on top of the base instructions for money-mindset sub-segments.

## Subjects that work
A quiet, reflective moment rather than a lesson: a mug steaming on a windowsill with a view
outside, an armchair by a window with a folded blanket, a phone face-down on a bedside table,
a single chair at a small table in soft morning light. If a figure appears, it is seen from
behind looking out of a window — relaxed, not anxious.

## Traps specific to this topic
- NO brains, lightbulbs, thought bubbles, hearts or meditation icons standing in for "mindset".
- No distress imagery at all: no head-in-hands, no tangled threads, no storm clouds.
- Nothing aspirational or glossy: no beaches, no luxury, no "financial freedom" imagery.
