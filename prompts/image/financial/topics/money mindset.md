These rules apply on top of the base instructions for money-mindset sub-segments.

These cards are about HOW IT FEELS to look at your money: avoidance, starting, being fair to
yourself about it. Choose the metaphor whose meaning matches the card.

## Metaphors (options, not a checklist)
- **Opening something you have been avoiding** — a curtain drawn back on a grey morning, a
  window pushed open onto a yard, a lamp switched on in a dim hallway. Fits cards about facing
  it, starting, looking properly for the first time.
- **A pause taken on purpose** — a chair turned toward a window with a mug on the sill, a back
  step with a cooling cup, a bus window seat with the world going past. Fits cards about
  slowing down, deciding without panic, letting a decision wait a day.
- **A room you can live in as it is** — an unmade bed with morning light across it, a hallway
  mid-tidy, a coat half hung. Fits cards about not needing to catch up, starting where you
  are, imperfect progress.

## Traps specific to this topic
- NO brains, lightbulbs, thought bubbles, hearts, or meditation icons standing in for
  "mindset".
- No distress imagery at all: no head-in-hands, no tangled threads, no storm clouds.
- Nothing aspirational or glossy: no beaches, no luxury, no "financial freedom" imagery.
