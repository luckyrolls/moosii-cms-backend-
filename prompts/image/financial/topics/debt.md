These rules apply on top of the base instructions for debt sub-segments.

## Subjects that work
Steady, practical progress: a short stack of opened envelopes neatly squared on a table
beside a mug, a folder with a pen resting on it, a wall calendar with a single day gently
circled (no numbers), a staircase or path seen from the side as a calm metaphor for
step-by-step progress.

## Traps specific to this topic
- NO imagery of weight, burden or danger: no chains, balls-and-chains, anchors, drowning,
  cliffs, heavy boulders, or storm clouds.
- NO red "overdue" or "final notice" stamps, no torn-open bills, no scattered papers, no one
  with their head in their hands. Debt is a practical problem to work through, never a shame.
- No collectors, knocking on doors, or phones ringing off the hook.
