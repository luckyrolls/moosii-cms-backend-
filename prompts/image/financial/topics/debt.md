These rules apply on top of the base instructions for debt sub-segments.

Debt cards are about ORDER, PACE and PROGRESS — never about weight or punishment. Choose the
metaphor whose meaning matches the card.

## Metaphors (options, not a checklist)
- **One step at a time** — a staircase with the lowest step lit by a hallway window, a garden
  path of stepping stones, a ladder leaning against a wall with one rung in the light. Fits
  cards about paying one debt first, steady progress, the first extra payment.
- **Choosing between similar things** — three coats on hooks with one taken down, two doors in
  a hallway with one ajar, a row of pans with one lifted from its hook. Fits cards about
  choosing an order (highest rate first, smallest balance first) or where extra money goes.
- **Untangling, calmly** — a garden hose half wound onto its reel, a cable being looped on a
  table, a knotted bootlace being worked loose on a back step. Fits cards about consolidation,
  simplifying several debts, or making sense of a tangle.

## Traps specific to this topic
- NO imagery of weight, burden or danger: no chains, balls-and-chains, anchors, drowning,
  cliffs, boulders, storm clouds.
- NO red "overdue" or "final notice" stamps, no torn-open bills, no scattered papers, no one
  with their head in their hands. Debt is a practical problem, never a shame.
- No collectors at the door, no ringing phones.
