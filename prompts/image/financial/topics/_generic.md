These rules apply on top of the base instructions for any topic without its own overlay.

## How to choose a scene
There is no metaphor list for this topic, so build the scene from the card's own words. Name
the moment the card describes — someone about to do the thing, or the room just after they did
— then place it: a setting, a light direction, a surface, three layers of depth.

## Traps
- Do not fall back to a desk with a notebook, or to a calendar. Ask where in a home or a day
  this card actually happens, and show that place.
- Keep people incidental (seen from behind, at a distance, hands only) or absent.
