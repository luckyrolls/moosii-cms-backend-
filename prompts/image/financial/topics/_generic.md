These rules apply on top of the base instructions for any topic without its own overlay.

Default subject: one ordinary object or corner of a home that fits the sub-segment — a table
with a folder and a mug, a window with a plant on the sill, a closed laptop on a desk. No
people unless the content is about an action, and then only hands or a figure seen from
behind. Register: calm, practical, unhurried.
