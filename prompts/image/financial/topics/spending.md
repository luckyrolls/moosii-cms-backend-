These rules apply on top of the base instructions for spending sub-segments.

Spending cards are usually about NOTICING something small that repeats. Choose the metaphor
whose meaning matches the card.

## Metaphors (options, not a checklist)
- **The small repeat you stop hearing** — a tap dripping into a shallow bowl in a kitchen, a
  window left ajar with the curtain moving, a radiator ticking in an empty hall. Fits cards
  about small recurring charges, subscriptions, anything that repeats unnoticed.
- **Duplicates you did not mean to own** — three near-identical jars in a cupboard, two
  half-used bottles of the same thing on a bathroom shelf, several tote bags on one hook. Fits
  cards about paying twice, overlapping services, the annual-versus-monthly trap.
- **A quiet review of the month** — a kitchen table after a meal with a phone face-down and a
  pen, a bus seat with a bag on the lap, an armchair with a closed laptop on its arm. Fits
  cards about scanning a month, deciding what to keep, cancelling something.

## Traps specific to this topic
- Receipts, screens, price tags and labels are BLANK — no numbers, no totals, no line items.
- NO shopping-spree imagery: no piles of bags, no overflowing carts, no sale signs.
- No guilt cues: no crossed-out items, no bare fridge, no worried figure over a bill.
