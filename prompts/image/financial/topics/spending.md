These rules apply on top of the base instructions for spending sub-segments.

## Subjects that work
Everyday purchases and noticing them: a grocery bag on a kitchen counter with a few simple
items (bread, fruit), a coffee cup to go on a bus seat, a phone face-down beside a set of
keys, a folded receipt shape on a table — blank, no printing. Reviewing spending can be a
calm tabletop scene: a notebook, a pen, a mug.

## Traps specific to this topic
- Receipts, screens and price tags are blank — no numbers, no totals, no line items.
- NO shopping-spree imagery: no piles of bags, no overflowing carts, no sale signs.
- No guilt cues: no crossed-out items, no empty fridge, no one looking worried at a bill.
