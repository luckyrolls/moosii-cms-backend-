These rules apply on top of the base instructions for accounts sub-segments.

Accounts cards are about WHERE money sits and how separate places become one view. Choose the
metaphor whose meaning matches the card.

## Metaphors (options, not a checklist)
- **Separate compartments, one piece of furniture** — a hallway unit of pigeonholes, a
  sewing box with divided trays, three blank tins in a row on a shelf. Fits cards about
  different accounts, separating money by purpose.
- **Everything visible at once** — a row of keys on hooks by a door, coats on a hall stand seen
  together, a window ledge with three plants in a line lit from one side. Fits cards about
  linking accounts and seeing everything in one place.
- **A door that opens between rooms** — an open doorway between a hall and a bedroom, a hatch
  in a wall, a gate in a garden fence. Fits cards about moving money between accounts,
  transfers, what connects to what.

## Traps specific to this topic
- NO bank buildings, storefronts, ATMs, or columns-and-pediment "bank" icons.
- NO app screens or dashboards: phones and laptops are face-down, closed, or dark.
- Tins, drawers and folders carry no labels, text or numbers.
