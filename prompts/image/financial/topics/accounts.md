These rules apply on top of the base instructions for accounts sub-segments.

## Subjects that work
Where money lives, shown as ordinary organisation: two or three plain folders standing on a
shelf, a small set of labelled-looking drawers with blank fronts, a phone face-down beside a
notebook, a key on a hook. Linking or separating accounts can be a simple metaphor — two
matching containers side by side on a table.

## Traps specific to this topic
- NO bank buildings, storefronts, ATMs, columns-and-pediment "bank" icons, or anything that
  reads as a specific institution.
- NO app screens or dashboards: phones and laptops are face-down, closed, or dark.
- Folders and drawers carry no labels, text or numbers.
