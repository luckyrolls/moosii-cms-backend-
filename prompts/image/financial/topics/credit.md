These rules apply on top of the base instructions for credit sub-segments.

## Subjects that work
Credit is abstract, so depict the ordinary moment around it: a wallet lying closed on a
table, a plain blank card half-tucked into a wallet, a bill envelope on a hallway shelf
beside keys, a phone face-down next to a calendar on the wall. Utilization and "limit vs
balance" ideas can be a simple container metaphor — a shelf with a few objects and plenty
of room left — never a meter.

## Traps specific to this topic
- NO score gauges, dials, speedometers, meters or rising arrows — the classic credit-score
  image is exactly what the base forbids.
- A card is a plain blank rectangle: no chip pattern that reads as a network, no numbers,
  no name, no logo, no brand colors.
- No lock-and-shield security imagery and no "approved/denied" stamps.
